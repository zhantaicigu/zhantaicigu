# Academic Profile

I am a Ph.D. researcher interested in intelligent control, robotics, and learning-based approaches to autonomous systems. My research interests span networked systems and the methods that connect control theory with embodied intelligence.

## Research Interests

- Robotics and intelligent control
- Multi-agent systems and distributed coordination
- Stochastic systems and containment control
- Humanoid robotics and motion planning
- Reinforcement learning and sim-to-real transfer
- Embodied AI and vision-language-action models
